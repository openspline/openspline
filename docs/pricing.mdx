title: "Pricing"
description: "Compare annual and monthly Openspline plans."
---

[View plans on Openspline Cloud →](https://app.openspline.dev/billing?cycle=monthly#plan-developer)

Monthly billing is selected by default: Developer is \$12/month, Scale is \$36/month, and Business is \$120/month. Annual billing is also available at \$120/year (\$10/month), \$360/year (\$30/month), and \$1,188/year (\$99/month), respectively. Taxes are calculated at checkout.

Annual plans grant credits monthly; each grant expires after 12 months. Monthly-plan credits expire each billing cycle. Existing monthly subscribers keep their original rates. Top-ups remain \$10 and never expire.
