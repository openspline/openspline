title: "Pricing"
description: "Compare annual and monthly Openspline plans."
---

[View plans on Openspline Cloud →](https://app.openspline.dev/billing?cycle=annual#plan-developer)

Annual billing is selected by default: Developer is $120/year ($10/month), Scale is $360/year ($30/month), and Business is $1,188/year ($99/month). New monthly subscriptions are $12, $36, and $120 respectively. Taxes are calculated at checkout.

Annual plans grant credits monthly; each grant expires after 12 months. Monthly-plan credits expire each billing cycle. Existing monthly subscribers keep their original rates. Top-ups remain $10 and never expire.
